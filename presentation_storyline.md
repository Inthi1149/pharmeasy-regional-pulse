\# PharmEasy Regional Pulse - Presentation Storyline



\## Flagship Finding



Guntur sales increased from INR 62,442.27 in April 2026 to INR 138,738.93 in May 2026, representing a +122.19% month-to-month increase. Sales then decreased to INR 99,745.18 in June 2026, a -28.11% change from May.



The dataset verifies these sales movements but does not establish their cause.



\---



\## 1. Executive Storyline - SCR



\### Situation



The PharmEasy Regional Pulse analysis reviews regional sales and profit performance across April, May, and June 2026. Month-to-month movements greater than 8% in absolute value are flagged for management review.



\### Complication



Guntur recorded the flagship April-to-May movement in the analysis. Sales increased from INR 62,442.27 in April to INR 138,738.93 in May, a +122.19% increase, before declining to INR 99,745.18 in June.



The size of the movement makes Guntur important to investigate, but the available dataset does not verify why the increase occurred. The 8% threshold is an operational-alert business rule for identifying material movements and is not a statistical-significance test.



\### Resolution



Management should review Guntur's underlying order volume, category mix, quantities, sales, and profit across April, May, and June. If campaign, promotion, inventory, or operational records are available, they should be compared with the May period before assigning a cause or taking action.



\---



\## 2. Regional Manager Storyline - OCD



\### Overview



Guntur is a priority region for review because sales changed materially across the three-month period. The April-to-May increase was +122.19%, followed by a -28.11% May-to-June movement.



\### Category



Use the dashboard's category breakdown to examine whether the sales movement is concentrated in particular product categories. Compare category sales and underlying order activity across April, May, and June rather than assuming that one category or external event caused the change.



\### Detail



Drill into Guntur's region/month detail and underlying orders. Review order counts, category mix, quantities, sales, and profit for the three months. Any explanation for the May increase should remain a hypothesis until supporting operational or commercial evidence is verified.

\## 3. Pushback Q\&A - Direct Acknowledgement Pattern



\### Q1. "Was the +122.19% increase caused by a promotion?"



\*\*Acknowledge:\*\* A promotion is a reasonable factor to investigate because the May increase is large.



\*\*Verified vs. not verified:\*\* What is verified is that Guntur sales increased from INR 62,442.27 in April to INR 138,738.93 in May, or +122.19%. The current dataset does not verify that a promotion caused the increase.



\*\*What resolves it / by when:\*\* Check available campaign and promotion records for Guntur against the May 2026 order data before the finding is used to make a promotion-related decision.



\### Q2. "Does crossing the 8% threshold mean the change is statistically significant?"



\*\*Acknowledge:\*\* The threshold identifies movements that deserve attention in this analysis.



\*\*Verified vs. not verified:\*\* The analysis verifies whether the absolute month-to-month percentage change exceeds 8%. It does not perform a statistical-significance test, so the 8% rule must not be interpreted as statistical significance.



\*\*What resolves it / by when:\*\* If statistical significance is required for a later decision, define an appropriate statistical method and supporting data before making that claim.



\### Q3. "Should we immediately act on the Guntur increase?"



\*\*Acknowledge:\*\* The +122.19% movement is large enough to justify prompt review.



\*\*Verified vs. not verified:\*\* The sales increase itself is verified, but its business cause is not established by the current analysis.



\*\*What resolves it / by when:\*\* Review the Guntur detail data and any available operational, inventory, campaign, or promotion records before approving an action based on a specific causal explanation.



\---



\## 4. Closing Message



The dashboard identifies where management attention is needed; it does not substitute an unverified explanation for evidence. Guntur's +122.19% April-to-May increase is the flagship finding, and the next step is to investigate the measured drivers and available business records before assigning a cause.
